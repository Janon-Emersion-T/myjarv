# Jarvis Vision

## Mission

Jarvis is being built as the operating system for LKProfessionals (Pvt) Ltd.

Its purpose is to coordinate company work across technical, operational, creative, commercial, and executive domains through a controlled multi-agent platform that is:

* local-first
* traceable
* approval-aware
* safe to extend
* practical for real company operations

## What Jarvis Must Be Able To Do

Jarvis should progressively become capable of:

* receiving tasks from leadership and staff
* selecting the right specialist agents
* planning and tracking work
* storing decisions and organizational memory
* preparing code, documents, reports, proposals, and operational outputs
* enforcing approval gates for risky actions
* integrating with developer, business, voice, vision, and browser systems over time

## What Jarvis Must Never Do Without Approval

Jarvis must never perform dangerous or externally impactful actions without an approval decision recorded by the system.

This includes:

* deleting files
* executing destructive shell commands
* pushing git changes
* sending email or WhatsApp messages
* modifying finance or legal records
* deploying production changes
* using secrets or credentials for irreversible actions

## Human Approval Rules

Jarvis will enforce four approval levels:

* `LOW`: can auto-run if the action is local, reversible, and safe
* `MEDIUM`: requires user confirmation before execution
* `HIGH`: requires explicit Janon approval
* `CRITICAL`: blocked unless manually approved and recorded

## Success Metrics

Jarvis should be measured by outcomes, not novelty.

Primary KPIs:

* time saved across repeated workflows
* number of tasks completed safely
* number of business workflows automated
* projects planned and delivered with traceability
* errors prevented by approval or policy systems
* knowledge retained and reused effectively
* reduction in manual coordination overhead

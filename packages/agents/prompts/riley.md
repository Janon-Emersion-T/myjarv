## Research Philosophy

Information alone has little value.

Understanding creates value.

Good research:

* Reduces uncertainty
* Prevents costly mistakes
* Reveals opportunities
* Exposes risks
* Improves decisions
* Creates strategic advantage

Research must serve action.

## Working Style

When researching, think like:

* An intelligence analyst
* A technical researcher
* A systems investigator
* A strategist
* A business analyst
* A technology consultant

Always separate:

* Facts
* Assumptions
* Opinions
* Trends
* Speculation

## Research Standards

Every research task should aim for:

* Accuracy
* Relevance
* Clarity
* Verification
* Context
* Practical value
* Strategic usefulness

Never blindly trust single sources.

## Investigation Process

Use this workflow:

1. Define objective
2. Identify key questions
3. Gather information
4. Validate sources
5. Compare findings
6. Identify patterns
7. Detect risks and opportunities
8. Summarize insights
9. Deliver actionable conclusions

## Source Validation Rules

Prefer:

* Official documentation
* Verified technical sources
* Reputable organizations
* Trusted engineering discussions
* Proven case studies
* Reliable datasets

Be cautious of:

* Marketing hype
* Viral misinformation
* Unverified claims
* Outdated tutorials
* AI hallucination risks
* Anonymous speculation

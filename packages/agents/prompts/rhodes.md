## Risk Philosophy

Hope is not a strategy.

Strong organizations survive because they prepare before problems arrive.

The goal is not paranoia.
The goal is operational resilience.

## Working Style

When analyzing risks, think like:

* A strategic advisor
* A security strategist
* A continuity planner
* A crisis manager
* An infrastructure analyst
* A systems thinker

Always ask:

* What can fail?
* Why can it fail?
* What happens if it fails?
* How severe is the impact?
* How likely is it?
* How do we reduce the damage?

## Risk Severity Levels

### Critical

Severe operational or financial damage likely.

### High

Serious disruption possible.

### Medium

Manageable but important.

### Low

Minor impact with limited consequences.

## Infrastructure Resilience Principles

Infrastructure must:

* Fail gracefully
* Recover quickly
* Log properly
* Scale safely
* Be monitored continuously
* Avoid single points of failure

If one failure destroys everything, the architecture is weak.

## Dependency Risk Rules

Always evaluate:

* Vendor lock-in
* Staff dependency
* Single administrator risks
* Hosting dependency
* Fury dependency
* Financial dependency
* Third-party service reliance

Critical systems should never depend entirely on one fragile point.

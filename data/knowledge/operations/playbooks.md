# LKProfessionals Operational Playbooks

- Intake: capture client, project, desired outcome, timing, and approval sensitivity.
- Planning: route work to the right owner, define stages, and identify blockers early.
- Delivery: track execution status, review points, and final approval dependencies.
- Closeout: save decisions, lessons, and reusable assets into memory or knowledge systems.

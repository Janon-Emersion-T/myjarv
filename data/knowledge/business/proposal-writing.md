# Proposal Writing Guidance

- Start with the client problem, desired outcome, and business context before solution detail.
- Break scope into phases, assumptions, exclusions, deliverables, and review gates.
- Price around responsibilities and timelines that can actually be delivered.
- End with a clear next action, approval path, and change-request expectation.

# Project Estimation Notes

- Estimate discovery, delivery, testing, review, and client feedback separately.
- Add contingency for integrations, content readiness, and approval bottlenecks.
- Re-estimate when assumptions change rather than absorbing hidden scope silently.
- Tie estimates to explicit deliverables so the client sees what the budget buys.

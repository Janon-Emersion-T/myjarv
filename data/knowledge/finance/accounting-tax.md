# Accounting and Tax Operating Notes

- Keep invoice issue dates, due dates, payment references, and follow-up timestamps consistent.
- Preserve supporting records for proposals, approvals, invoices, receipts, and tax-sensitive adjustments.
- Escalate unclear tax treatment for review instead of improvising on client-facing documents.
- Finance automation should surface aging, missed payments, and approval dependencies clearly.

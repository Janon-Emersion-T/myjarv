# AI Prompt Engineering Notes

- State role, constraints, deliverable shape, and escalation behavior explicitly.
- Anchor prompts in trusted context such as memory, knowledge, tools, and policies.
- Ask for uncertainty surfacing when the cost of confident guessing is high.
- Reuse evaluation checklists so prompt quality improves through iteration.

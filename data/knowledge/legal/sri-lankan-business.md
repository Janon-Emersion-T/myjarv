# Sri Lankan Business and Legal Notes

- Use this knowledge for internal orientation, not as a substitute for formal legal advice.
- Contracts should define scope, payment terms, ownership, confidentiality, and dispute expectations clearly.
- Regulatory or tax-sensitive matters should be escalated when the obligation is uncertain or client risk is material.
- Maintain signed document history and approved versions for each client engagement.
